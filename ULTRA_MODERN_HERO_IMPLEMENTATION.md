### 4. **Responsive Design**

✅ **Desktop (1440px+)**: Full features enabled
✅ **Laptop (1024-1439px)**: Slightly reduced sizes
✅ **Tablet (768-1023px)**: Stacked layout, simplified animations
✅ **Mobile (<768px)**: Single column, no right side, simplified particles
✅ **Extra Small (<480px)**: Further size reductions

### 5. **Performance Optimizations**

- ✅ GPU-accelerated transforms (transform/opacity)
- ✅ Reduced particles on mobile (20 vs 45)
- ✅ Disabled parallax on mobile
- ✅ Debounced mouse listeners
- ✅ IntersectionObserver for animations
- ✅ Will-change used sparingly
- ✅ Lazy loading ready

### 6. **Accessibility Features**

- ✅ Semantic HTML structure
- ✅ ARIA labels on interactive elements
- ✅ Keyboard navigation support
- ✅ Focus indicators (champagne-gold outline)
- ✅ Reduced motion support (prefers-reduced-motion)
- ✅ High contrast mode support
- ✅ Touch targets minimum 44x44px
- ✅ Screen reader friendly

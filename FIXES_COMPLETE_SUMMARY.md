## 💡 Key Improvements Explained

### 1. Image Clarity (40% Overlay)
**Why 40%?** Gradient only covers the area where content sits, allowing 60% of the image to be fully visible for maximum visual impact.

### 2. Larger Destination Names (32px)
**Why 32px?** At 32px with strong shadow, the destination name becomes the primary focal point, ensuring users immediately know the destination.

### 3. Enhanced Info Pills
**Why stronger styles?** More opacity (0.25), blur (12px), and border (0.4) make the information readable without distracting from the image.

### 4. Proper Grid Spacing (24px)
**Why 24px?** Creates breathing room between cards while maintaining visual connection. Reduces from 28px which felt cramped.

### 5. Premium Interactions
**Why better hover effects?** Small improvements (image zoom 1.1x, shadow +25%) make interactions feel premium and responsive.
